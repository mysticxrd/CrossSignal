# Three-minute Results Team story

## One-page demo claim

> CrossSignal turns a deterministic, versioned fixture signal into an
> inspectable decision record: EVE performs bounded durable work, ICM exposes
> the exact human-editable semantic state, D1 independently authorizes and
> records operations, and no sandbox effect or Company Brain change occurs
> without a current human decision.

This proves the ICM + EVE mechanism. It does not claim live customer
integrations, production schedules, or production GitHub writes.
