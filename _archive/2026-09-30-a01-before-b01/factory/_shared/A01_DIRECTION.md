# A01 direction and preserved invariants

The user's correction and subsequent “continue” select one complete synthetic
Revenue Leak Investigator Case as the next governed run. The human-readable
source is `docs/a01-synthetic-investigation-handoff.md`; the current Spec
records its exact hash. The Notion hiring proposal, especially section 24,
is linked and reconciled in `docs/hiring-mvp-direction.md`.

The Case connects a reproducible incident, two or three explanations,
bounded domain tools, meaningful contradiction search, remedy or abstention,
exact human approval, a simulated intervention, observed synthetic result,
durable timeline, and separate hidden-truth grading.

Use A01: a deployment affects Indian debit cards. Preserve Mobile Safari
as regression coverage. Real payment integrations, device verification,
business recovery, generic governance expansion, and a 100-case benchmark
are not prerequisites for this bounded run.

Deterministic code owns validation, metrics, authority, execution reservation,
and grading. A bounded investigator owns evidence selection and hypothesis
updates. A scripted baseline proves mechanics only. Model quality needs a
separate recorded local-model evaluation; if unavailable, report that gap.
No paid model calls.

Preserve tenant isolation, exact payload/context/policy approval, Global
Pause, one-time effect reservation, explicit indeterminate states, append-only
history, and the excluded trial receipt. Do not reuse a manual-check approval
for rollback. No LIVE writes.

All outcome surfaces say SIMULATED. No claim of real payment capture,
Mobile Safari verification, or recovered business revenue. Evaluator answers,
private simulator state, and authored scenario labels are inaccessible to the
investigator and domain tools. Separate process placement alone is not proof
of access isolation.

After A01 passes, recommend five and ten causally different incidents and
baseline comparisons. The factory remains an internal process: do not promote
historical L14 acceptance or stage counts into hiring-MVP completion.
